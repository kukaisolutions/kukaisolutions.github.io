This guide shows what to set up before you start using Beiwe in your clinic's day-to-day work. Setup is done by an **administrator**, and each step takes a few minutes.

🎬 Start with the video [Introducing Beiwe: the platform's guiding threads](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hTIwWpbMZ7w) (in Portuguese). It shows how the pieces fit together.

Some Beiwe screens are still only in Portuguese. When a label on screen is in Portuguese, this guide shows it in brackets, like this: **Service name** (*Nome do Serviço*).

## How the pieces fit together

- **Services** (for example, Occupational Therapy) and their **statuses** (Scheduled, Completed…) are the basis of the schedule and the finances.
- **Locations** say where each appointment takes place.
- **Document structures** define the reports the team writes in the patient records.
- **Collaborators** are the team that sees patients; **patients** receive the appointments, and each one has their own price per service.
- With all that in place, you book **appointments** in the Schedule. When an appointment is completed, it goes into the finances and can get a report.

## The recommended order

| Step | Where | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Add the service locations | Settings → Service Locations | [How to add a location](/products/beiwe/en/add-location/) |
| 2. Create the document structures (reports and diaries) | Settings → Document Structures | [How to create a report structure](/products/beiwe/en/create-document-structure/) |
| 3. Add the services and their statuses | Settings → Services Provided | [How to add a service and its statuses](/products/beiwe/en/add-service-and-statuses/) |
| 4. Register the team | Registration → Collaborators | [How to register a collaborator](/products/beiwe/en/add-collaborator/) |
| 5. Register the patients and their prices | Registration → Patients | [How to register a patient and their prices](/products/beiwe/en/add-patient-and-prices/) |
| 6. Book the appointments | Schedule | [How to book an appointment](/products/beiwe/en/schedule-appointment/) |

Services come before patients because each patient's price is set per service.

After that, the team can write reports in the patient records, and can even dictate them: see [How to write reports with AI voice transcription](/products/beiwe/en/report-with-ai-transcription/).

## Start with your own profile

When the clinic's account is created, the administrator appears as a collaborator with the temporary name "ADMIN". On the first sign-in, the **Home** page shows a notice with a link to complete your name, role and other details in **Registration → Collaborators**.

🎬 Video: [First sign-in and registering administrators](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dHRN0TTfIHQ) (in Portuguese)

## The Home page tells you what is missing

While any essential setting is missing, the **Home** page shows notices with a link to the right section. For example: no service registered, a service without statuses, no location, or no document type. When the notices are gone, the basic setup is complete.

## Optional settings

They don't stop you from using Beiwe, but they make it look like your clinic:

- **Display name** (*Nome de Exibição na Beiwe*, in Settings → Company Information): the name shown at the top of the pages.
- **Company logo** (Settings → Company Logo): shown in the menu and on documents, invoices and reports. JPG or PNG.
- **Bank details and Pix** (*Dados Bancários*, in Settings → Company Information): used on the invoices and payment slips in Finance.
- **Document storage folders** (*Pastas de armazenamento de documentos*, in Settings): where the files sent through "Upload New Files" (*Upload de Novas Informações*) in the patient records are kept, and who each folder is shared with.
- **Access** (*Acessos*, in Settings): shows what each profile (administrator, collaborator, patient/guardian) sees in Beiwe. Extra access for a collaborator is granted in Registration → Collaborators → Edit Collaborator.
