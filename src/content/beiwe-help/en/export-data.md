Before you close the account, download a copy of everything your clinic needs to keep. After the export period, the data is deleted and can't be recovered, neither by your team nor by Kukai Solutions.

Only the clinic's **administrators** can export the data.

## What you can download

The export brings together all the **files** stored in Beiwe, organized in folders:

- **One folder per patient**, named with the patient's name and ID (for example, `pacientes/Maria Souza (P000012)/`). Inside it are:
  - the **evolution reports** created in Beiwe (signed PDFs), separated by service and by date;
  - the **diaries** (the entries are in a `diary_entries.csv` file, which opens in Excel or Google Sheets);
  - the **files sent** through "Upload New Files" (*Upload de Novas Informações*): documents, test results, photos, videos and audio, separated by the folder they were saved in and by date.
- **Other company folders**, such as `legal/` (the records of acceptance of the Terms of Use and other legal documents) and `images/` (the clinic's logo).

The files come out **already decrypted** and with the names shown in Beiwe, ready to open on your computer.

## Step by step

1. Sign in to Beiwe with an administrator account and open **My Account**.
2. Go to **☁️ Storage Usage** and open **📂 Storage per Patient**.
3. Tick the folders you want to download. To take everything, use the **Select all** box at the top of each table: the patients' table and the **Other company folders (download only)** table.
4. Click **⬇️ Download**. You get a ZIP file named after the clinic and today's date.
5. Unzip it and check that the files open.

Large folders (with many videos or audio files, for example) may take a few minutes to download. If the clinic has many patients, you can download in batches, a few patients at a time.

If a file can't be included, the ZIP contains an **ERROS.txt** file listing it. In that case, try downloading that folder again or write to contato@kukaisolutions.com.

### A single patient's record

To download a single patient's record, open **Patient Records**, choose the patient and use **📥 Patient record download** (*Download de prontuário*). In the same section, **Combine reports into a single PDF** (*Combinar relatórios em um único PDF*) puts the patient's reports together in one document.

## What the files don't include

The clinic's **registrations and records** are kept in Beiwe's database, not in the folders: patient and guardian registration, the schedule and appointment notes, financial data, the team and the settings. They are not part of the ZIP.

If you need a copy of these records, ask contato@kukaisolutions.com **within the export period**.

## Deadlines

- After the contract ends, the clinic has **120 days** to export its data (clause 10.4 of the Terms of Use). During that period, administrators can still open **My Account** to download the files.
- Kukai emails the administrators a reminder every week, and again 7 days and 1 day before the deadline.
- Once the period ends, access is blocked and the data is permanently deleted (clause 10.5): files within 60 days, and the other records within 365 days.
- You can also ask for the data to be deleted earlier, after ending the contract (clause 10.5.1).

## Before closing: keeping patient records

Professional councils require patient records and clinical documents to be kept for several years (in Brazil, the Federal Council of Psychology, CFP, requires at least 5 years). This period is usually longer than the export period. Keeping the records is the clinic's responsibility, so export and store safely everything you need to keep.

## How to close the account

Once the data is saved, write to **contato@kukaisolutions.com** asking to close the account.

Questions? Write to us at contato@kukaisolutions.com.
