Appointments are booked in Beiwe's **Schedule**, which is linked to the clinic's Google Calendar: each appointment becomes an event, and Beiwe can send invitations to the collaborator and the guardians.

🎬 Video: [How to book appointments](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4-zK_T0ab0Y) (in Portuguese)

First, register at least one location, one service with statuses, one collaborator and one patient (see [Getting started](/products/beiwe/en/getting-started/)).

## Step by step

1. Open **Schedule** and go to **✏️ Create and delete appointments** (*Criar e deletar consultas*).
2. Open **➕ New event** (*Novo evento*) and choose the type:
   - **🩺 Service** (*Serviço*): an appointment with a patient;
   - **✅ Task** (*Tarefa*): a team commitment without a patient (meeting, supervision). The title is required.
3. **Title** (*Título*): for appointments, we recommend leaving it **blank**. That way Google Calendar shows a title that doesn't identify the patient (for example, "Psychotherapy · Room 2 · Beiwe"), while Beiwe shows the patient's and the collaborator's names. Avoid typing the patient's name in the title.
4. Enter the **Date** (*Data*, YYYY-MM-DD), the **Start** (*Início*) and the **End** (*Fim*) times (HH:MM).
5. Choose the **Patient** (*Paciente*), the **Collaborator** (*Colaborador*), the **Service** (*Serviço*), the **Status** (usually the scheduled one) and the **Location** (*Local*).
6. Under **Send invitation to guardians** (*Enviar convite aos responsáveis*), tick who should get the invitation. Only guardians with "Receive progress updates and appointment invitations" turned on in the patient's registration appear here.
7. Write **Notes** (*Notas*), if you want.
8. Click **➕ Create event** (*Criar evento*).

## Recurring appointments

To book a series of appointments at once, open **🔁 Schedule recurring appointments** (*Agendar consultas periódicas*):

- **By time span** (*Por intervalo de tempo*): repeats every so many **weeks**, for a number of **months** (up to 6), starting on the appointment date;
- **By end date** (*Por data final*): repeats every so many weeks, from the appointment date **until** the date you choose.

## Notifications and invitations

- **Send invitation to collaborator** (*Enviar convite ao colaborador*): sends a calendar invitation to the collaborator's email.
- **Google Calendar notification** (*Notificação no Google Calendar*): a reminder before the appointment (10 min, 30 min or 1 hour), for the invited collaborator.

## After the appointment

When the session has taken place, open the appointment in the Schedule, under **Edit event** (*Editar evento*), and change the status to the completed one (or another billable status). From then on it counts as an amount receivable in **Finance**, and the appointment's report can be written in Patient Records.
