*Prices in effect on November 24, 2026. Amounts are in Brazilian reais (R$).*

With Beiwe, the clinic pays per **seat**: each person who uses the platform takes up one seat, and the price depends on their profile.

## Price per seat, per month

| Profile | Starter plan | Regular plan |
|---|---|---|
| **Administrator**: sets up the clinic, registers the team and the patients, uses Finance | R$ 70.00 | R$ 80.00 |
| **Collaborator**: therapists and other professionals who see patients and write reports | R$ 20.00 | R$ 25.00 |
| **Guardian with access to the patient record**: a patient or family member who signs in to Beiwe to see the record | R$ 3.00 | R$ 3.00 |

Guardians who only receive reports and invitations by email, without signing in to Beiwe, **don't take up a seat**.

### Example

A clinic with 1 administrator, 3 collaborators and 10 guardians with access to the patient record pays, per month:

| | Starter plan | Regular plan |
|---|---|---|
| 1 administrator | R$ 70.00 | R$ 80.00 |
| 3 collaborators | R$ 60.00 | R$ 75.00 |
| 10 guardians | R$ 30.00 | R$ 30.00 |
| **Total** | **R$ 160.00** | **R$ 185.00** |

## AI transcription

Voice transcription turns the professional's dictation into text, already split into the report's sections.

- **120 minutes per month included** for the clinic, at no extra cost.
- Beyond that: **R$ 0.08 per minute**, charged for the exact time used (2.4 minutes cost 2.4 minutes, with no rounding up).
- Usage is capped at **240 minutes per month** per clinic. In Settings, the administrator chooses the clinic's cap, between 120 and 240 minutes.

## Trial period

You can try Beiwe **free for 2 months**, with no charge for seats or transcription.

## How payment works

- **Prepaid, with credits via Pix.** The clinic adds credits in My Account, via Pix. Each access period is debited from the credit balance.
- **Access by monthly period.** Each payment unlocks Beiwe from the start day until the day before the same day of the following month. With automatic renewal turned on and enough balance, the next period is debited automatically.
- **New seats in the middle of a period** are charged only for the days left until the end of the period. The screen shows the amount before you confirm.
- **Unused credits don't expire** and can be refunded on request to contato@kukaisolutions.com.
- **Grace period:** if the period ends without enough balance, access continues for 3 days so the clinic can add credits without losing its renewal date.
- **Pause:** the clinic can pause access for up to 60 days in total every 12 months (for example, during holidays). The remaining paid days are kept, and there is no seat charge during the pause: only storage above 5 GB is charged, at R$ 0.50 per GB per month, debited when the pause starts.
- **Amounts are always truncated to the cent**, never rounded up.
- **Price changes** are announced at least 60 days in advance.

## Cancellation

The clinic can end its contract at any time by writing to contato@kukaisolutions.com. After cancellation, the data remains available for export for 120 days. See [How to export your clinic's data](/products/beiwe/en/export-data/).
