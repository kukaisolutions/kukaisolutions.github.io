With AI transcription, the professional **dictates** the report instead of typing it. Beiwe transcribes the audio and splits the text into the document's sections. The professional reviews it and generates the PDF.

🎬 Video: [How to write reports with AI voice transcription](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fFzwQqI4u0U) (in Portuguese)

## Before you start

- The administrator turns AI on for each person by ticking **Access to AI tools** (*Acesso a ferramentas de IA*) in **Registration → Collaborators** (see [How to register a collaborator](/products/beiwe/en/add-collaborator/)). Without it, the AI area shows as locked 🔒.
- The document needs a structure with sections (see [How to create a report structure](/products/beiwe/en/create-document-structure/)).
- **Don't record sessions without the consent of the patient or their guardian.** Transcription is meant for dictating the report.

## Step by step

1. Open **Patient Records** (*Prontuários*), choose the patient and go to **✍️ Create documents → Evolution Reports** (*Criar documentos → Relatórios de Evolução*).
2. Fill in the document's basic details: service, document structure, collaborator and date. The video [How to finish an appointment and write a report](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vo3AN5XL-dg) (in Portuguese) shows this part.
3. Under **🎙️🤖 Voice filling (AI)** (*Preenchimento por voz (IA)*), tick in **Fields to fill** (*Campos a preencher*) the sections the AI should fill in.
4. Record your dictation with **🎙️ Record audio** (*Gravar áudio*) and stop with **⏹️ Stop** (*Parar*), or upload an audio file.
5. Click **🧠 Transcribe and fill** (*Transcrever e preencher*) and wait a few seconds.
6. The raw text appears in the **📝 Transcription** (*Transcrição*) box, and the ticked sections are filled in.
7. **Always review** the text of each section and correct whatever needs it.
8. Click **📄 Generate PDF** (*Gerar PDF*).

The same AI area also appears in **diaries**.

## Dictating in parts

You can record or upload several audio files, one at a time. If a section already has text, Beiwe asks what to do:

- **➕ Append to the current text** (*Acrescentar ao texto atual*): the new text goes after what is already written, and nothing is deleted;
- **🔄 Replace with the new transcription** (*Substituir pela nova transcrição*): the new text takes the place of what was written in those sections.

That way, you can build the report bit by bit.

## Transcription or section text?

The AI adjusts the text to fit it into the sections, so the section text may differ from the raw transcription. If the clinic prefers to keep the professional's own words, copy the passage from the **📝 Transcription** box and paste it into the section, editing as needed. The raw transcription can also contain mistakes: check names, dates and technical terms.

## Accepted audio

- Formats: MP3, M4A, WAV, OGG, FLAC, MP4 and MPGA, up to 25 MB. Audio recorded on the page itself is already in an accepted format.
- Length: up to about 25 minutes per file for recordings made on the page, WhatsApp audio, regular MP3 and phone M4A. WAV files take up more space and fit only a few minutes. For longer dictations, split the audio into parts and use **➕ Append to the current text**.

## Transcription minutes

The clinic has **120 minutes per month included**. The administrator can raise the cap to up to 240 minutes in **Settings → AI transcription limits** (*Limites de transcrição por IA*), and choose whether transcription is blocked when the cap is reached. Minutes beyond the included ones are charged (see [Pricing](/products/beiwe/en/pricing/)). The AI area shows how much has been used in the period.
