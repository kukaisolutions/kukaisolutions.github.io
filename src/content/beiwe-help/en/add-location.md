Locations say **where** each appointment takes place: a room, a clinic branch, the patient's school, an online session. The schedule needs at least one location.

🎬 Video: [Setting up locations](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Yp860GZa5XU) (in Portuguese)

## Step by step

1. Sign in to Beiwe as an **administrator** and open **Settings**.
2. Go to **📍 Service Locations**.
3. In **Location name** (*Nome do Local*), type the name (for example, "Room 1", "Downtown Branch" or "Online").
4. Click **Add Location**.

The location appears in the **Registered Locations** list and can now be chosen when you book an appointment in the Schedule.

## Tips

- Add one location for each room or address you want to tell apart in the schedule. In the Schedule, you can filter appointments by location and color the calendar by location.
- The location's name appears in the Google Calendar event title when the title is left blank (for example, "Psychotherapy · Room 2 · Beiwe"). So don't put patient details in a location's name.
- To remove a location, click 🗑️ next to it in the list.
