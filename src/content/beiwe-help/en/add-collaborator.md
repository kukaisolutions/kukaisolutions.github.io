**Collaborators** are the people on your team: therapists, psychologists, speech therapists and other professionals. Once registered, they can be chosen in the schedule, sign in to Beiwe and write reports.

🎬 Video: [How to add collaborators](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bDFVtA5hkyU) (in Portuguese)

## Before you start: the person creates their own account

Beiwe only registers collaborators who **already have an account**. Ask the person to:

1. open the Beiwe sign-in page and create an account with their email address;
2. confirm the email address through the link they receive.

If the account doesn't exist yet, Beiwe warns you: "O usuário não teve seu email registrado ainda" (the user's email has not been registered yet).

## Step by step

1. Sign in to Beiwe as an **administrator** and open **Registration → Collaborators**.
2. Under **Register New Collaborator**, fill in:
   - **Full name** (*Nome completo*) and **Role** (*Cargo*). The roles are listed in Portuguese: occupational therapist, speech therapist, psychologist, physiotherapist, educator, or other;
   - **Email**: the same one the person used to create their account. It can't be changed later;
   - **Phone** (*Telefone*), **Regional council name** (*Nome do conselho regional*, for example CREFITO) and **Council number** (*Número no conselho*), if any;
   - **Signature on reports** (*Assinatura em relatórios*): the text that goes at the end of the reports this person writes;
   - **Access to AI tools** (*Acesso a ferramentas de IA*), if the person may use AI transcription (see [How to write reports with AI voice transcription](/products/beiwe/en/report-with-ai-transcription/)).
3. If you want, record the person's **certifications** (name, notes, issue and expiry dates) and click **Add Certification**.
4. Click **Register Collaborator**.

Each collaborator takes up one **seat** in the plan. Before you confirm, the screen shows how much will be debited from your credits.

## What the collaborator sees

The collaborator signs in to Beiwe with the **Collaborator** profile. By default, they see only the patients they have appointments with, can change only their own appointments, and write reports. They can't open Finance or the patient registration.

To grant more, after registering the person, choose them under **Edit Collaborator** and tick the **🔐 Granted access** (*Acessos liberados*) options:

- **All patients and schedules** (*Todos os pacientes e agendas*);
- **Patient and guardian registration** (*Cadastro de pacientes e responsáveis*): register, edit and deactivate, but not delete;
- **Finance** (*Financeiro*): the whole page;
- **Prices and payments in Patient Records** (*Preços e pagamentos em Prontuários*).

Changes take effect within a minute. The full table of what each profile sees is in **Settings → Access** (*Acessos*).

## Editing later

Under **Edit Collaborator**, choose the person to change their name, phone, council, signature and certifications. This is also where the administrator completes their own profile on the first sign-in, replacing the temporary name "ADMIN" with their own name.
